How bodyweight training overloads

Every argument against calisthenics is the same one: you can’t add weight, so you can’t progress.

It is wrong — and wrong in a useful way.

Chapter 2 · Push

You can’t add weight. You don’t need to.

Move your body further from the pivot and the same muscle works harder. Your weight never changed.

Hands on a waist-high bar: about 40 % of your bodyweight. On the floor: about 65 %. Feet up on a chair: about 75 %.

Remember Leverage is your weight plate.

Chapter 7 · Progress

When to move up

Beat the top of your rep range by two, twice in a row. Then step up.

Your reps will drop when you do. That is the system working, not you going backwards.

The rule Two extra reps. Two sessions. Step up.

If form breaks, stay where you are. A rep only counts if you’d be happy to film it.

Why
