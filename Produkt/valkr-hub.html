      Skills

      These are why most people start calisthenics. Each one has an entry requirement — the hub checks yours against it.

      Three rules for skill work

      • Train it fresh, right after the warm-up, before anything else.
      • Stop well short of failure. Skills are coordination, and tired practice builds a sloppy pattern.
      • Ten minutes four or five times a week beats an hour once.

      Find your starting point

      Four tests, twenty minutes. Warm up first, rest three minutes between each, and stop the moment your form breaks. Your lowest score is your level.
